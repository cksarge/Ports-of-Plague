# Ports of Plague — Balance Simulation

Generated by `npm run simulate` on 2026-10-01. Each game uses randomly chosen home cities and randomly assigned computer strategies.

**Strategies.** *Greedy*: always ships for the most money, never gives or flees. *Cautious*: moves family away from danger first, avoids risky cargo. *Charitable*: gives to charity every turn and accepts offers. *Balanced*: works on its weakest score category. *Random*: picks any legal move (a stand-in for a confused beginner).

**Time model (players at a decent pace).** 10 s to choose each action and 6 s per decision (together capped at 30 s per turn by the turn timer), 2.5 s per shipment result (clean shipments close by themselves), 10 s per Fortune card, 5 s to change player, 10 s per round banner, 20 s per Chronicle page (up to 4 cards), 15 s per Event card, 15 s per plague card (2 s when nobody rolls) and 60 s for the turn-order roll. Pre-plague rounds on. First games and reading every card aloud will run longer.
